Submitting a claim

  1. Take a photo or make a pdf of your receipt or invoice.
  2. {% if mailbox %}Email the receipt to {{ mailbox }}, or{% else %}Log in and{% endif %} add it in the portal via New claim. Several receipts in one email become one claim.
  3. You receive a confirmation email with a personal link.
  4. Use that link to fill in the amount, a description, your IBAN and the account holder. Keep "remember" ticked and your bank details will be filled in next time (you can always change them).
  5. You get an email at every step: approved, payment prepared, paid, or rejected with the reason. The same link always shows the status.

You can change your details until the chair has approved the claim. No email? Check your spam folder.

Chair: approve and initial

  1. You get an email when a claim is waiting for you. Open it in the portal, or look under My tasks.
  2. The receipt is shown right on the page. Check the amount and description.
  3. Click Approve and initial. Every receipt gets a stamp with your name, the date and the reference; the initialled pdf is then attached to the claim.
  4. Something wrong? Choose Reject and give a reason. The submitter will see that reason.

If the administrator has turned on approve via a link for you, the email also contains a link to approve directly, without logging in. The link opens the claim with the receipts; it is only approved once you click Approve and initial. Each link works once and expires after a few days. To reject or add a comment, log in.

Treasurer: prepare the payment

  1. After approval you get an email with amount, IBAN, account holder, description and reference, with the initialled receipts attached.
  2. Prepare the payment at the bank and use the reference in the payment description.
  3. Click Payment has been prepared in the portal. The secretary is then notified.

Secretary: make the payment

  1. You get an email as soon as the treasurer has prepared the payment.
  2. Make the payment at the bank.
  3. Click Payment has been made in the portal. The claim is then marked as paid and everyone, including the submitter, knows it has been paid.

Creating a claim for someone else

Every staff member can enter a claim via New claim, for example for a paper receipt.

  • Enter the submitter's email address and add the receipt(s).
  • If you also fill in amount, description, IBAN and account holder, the claim goes straight to the chair.
  • If you leave them empty, the submitter receives an email with a link to complete them.

Administration

  • Board members and administrators have the Settings menu at the top right. Chair, treasurer and secretary see Users (view only), Log and Notifications in it. The administrator also sees Mail settings and Updates. Members with only the Member role don't see this menu.
  • With the Administrator role you can add people under Users, assign roles, deactivate accounts and set a new password.
  • Give members who want to submit claims themselves the Member role. They can log in, create a claim and only see their own claims.
  • When the board changes, create an account for the new board member and deactivate the old one. History is kept.
  • Under Updates you see which version is running and whether a newer one exists. Update now makes the server fetch the latest version from GitHub and restart the tool; this takes a few minutes.
  • Under Mail settings you set up the mailbox the tool uses to send mail and fetch receipts.
  • Under Notifications every board member chooses whether to get an e-mail for a new claim, for every status change and when a task has been open for longer than a chosen number of days (3 by default). That last one is sent only once per task. Administrators can also switch on an e-mail when someone logs in, when logging in is temporarily blocked and when mail arrives. Everything is off by default.
  • Under Log you see who signed in when, which sign-in attempts failed (with reason and IP address) and which mail came in and what happened to it. Old entries are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • An administrator can edit the details of any claim (even after approval) and permanently delete a claim, for example to clean up test data. Deleting also removes the attachments and the history.
  • Chair, treasurer and secretary can turn on Simple view under their name: after signing in they only see what is waiting for them, with large buttons and no settings. An administrator can also switch it on per person under Users.
  • An administrator can use View as under Users to see the portal exactly as that person sees it, for example to help the chair over the phone. A bar at the top shows whose view you are in. Approving, saving or sending anything is not possible meanwhile. Stop viewing takes you back to your own account. Each view is recorded in the log under Sign-ins.
  • Board members and administrators can add internal notes to a claim. These are never visible to the submitter.
  • Everyone can change their language and password under their own name (top right).
  • Everyone can turn on two-step verification under their own name (top right): after your password you then also enter a code from an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator or Bitwarden) or from an e-mail. Keep the recovery codes safe. Lost both your phone and your recovery codes? Ask the administrator to reset two-step verification.
  • Under Updates you can also see when the last backup succeeded. Every night an encrypted backup is sent to Google Drive. If it stops working, you see a warning on the overview.

Security and privacy

The tool stores names, bank account numbers and receipts. This is how we keep them safe:

  • The tool has no access to the bank. Nothing is ever paid automatically. The treasurer prepares a payment at the bank and the secretary makes it there: always two people.
  • Everyone can only take the step that belongs to their own role. Only the chair approves, only the secretary reports that it has been paid. An administrator cannot skip this either. Every step is recorded with name and time.
  • Anyone without an account only sees the login screen. The site is not listed in search engines.
  • The connection is encrypted (https), just like online banking.
  • Passwords are stored in unreadable form. After 5 wrong attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. All login attempts are logged.
  • A submitter gets a personal link with a code that cannot be guessed, and only sees their own claim with it. Internal board comments are never shown to the submitter.
  • Attachments can only be a PDF or a photo.
  • Every night an encrypted backup is sent to a separate place outside the server. It can only be opened with the administrator's key.
  • The server is in Germany, within the EU. It has a firewall, security updates are installed automatically and the database cannot be reached from outside.

What you can do yourself

  • Choose a long password you don't use anywhere else, for example a short sentence.
  • Turn on two-step verification under your own name (top right). It takes a minute and is strongly recommended for board members.
  • Don't forward a personal link; anyone who has it can see your claim.
  • Something not right, like an e-mail about a login you don't recognise? Tell the administrator.

As an app on your phone

You can put the claims tool on your phone's home screen as an app. It then opens full screen, without an address bar. No app store is needed, and after an update you get the new version straight away.

  • Android (Chrome): open the tool, tap the menu (⋮) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • iPhone and iPad (Safari): open the tool, tap the share button (square with an arrow) and choose Add to Home Screen.

Count on the icon: when tasks are waiting for you, the app icon shows how many. On iPhone and iPad, switch this on once under your name (top right) with Show count on app icon. The count is updated when you open the app. Android does not show a count on the icon.

The app only works online: your claims and bank details are not stored on your phone. In the app you log in once separately from your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Which files can I send?
Pdf, jpg, png, webp and heic (iPhone photos), up to {{ settings.max_upload_mb }} MB per file. If the browser can't show a heic photo on the page, open it via the link.
Why do I get reminders?
Every morning everyone with an open task gets one summary email. Submitters who haven't completed their details also get a reminder.
How do I change the language?
Without logging in via NL/EN at the top right; when logged in under your own name (top right).
Forgot your password?
Ask an administrator to set a new password via Users.
